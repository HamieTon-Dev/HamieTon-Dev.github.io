CyOps TD — Privacy Policy

Developer: HamieTon.dev · Effective: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game CyOps TD ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game itself has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own. Information leaves your device only through Google services — advertising, Google Play purchases, and, if you choose to link it, Google Play Games.

1. Information the game stores on your device

Your progress — waves reached, statistics, unlocked agents, in-game currency, settings, cosmetic choices and a saved run — is stored in the game's private storage on your device. The game may also store a callsign you choose (3–16 characters, letters A–Z, digits, dash and underscore only). The callsign cannot contain spaces or symbols, so it cannot hold an email address or phone number. Uninstalling the game, or using RESET PROGRESS in Settings, deletes this information from the device.

Android's own backup service may back this data up to your Google account and restore it on a new device, under Google's terms.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob in two places: an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to continue a lost run, and an ad after a lost run. Buying REMOVE ADS removes the ad after a lost run.

Because the game's audience includes children and the game does not ask anyone's age, every ad request is treated as child-directed: ads are non-personalized, limited to a G content rating, and the game does not use the Android Advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app). Google may still collect limited information to serve and measure ads, such as:

  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • crash and diagnostic information from the ads software.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. If you decline, no ads are requested. Where required, a PRIVACY OPTIONS entry in Settings lets you change your choice at any time.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details, name or address. It receives only a confirmation and an order number from Google Play, which it keeps on the device so a purchase is not credited twice. Refunds and receipts are handled by Google Play.

4. Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to link your Google account in the game's GOOGLE PLAY screen, the game uses Google Play Games Services to:

  • Save your progress to your own Google account (the same progress listed in section 1), so it can be restored on another device;
  • Post to the global leaderboard: the wave you reached, the damage dealt in that run, and your callsign. Other players can see your leaderboard entry, including your callsign and your Google Play Games display name.

Linking is optional and the game is fully playable without it. Google Play Games is provided by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

5. What the game does not do

  • No account, login or password of its own.
  • No analytics or tracking by the developer, and no data sold by the developer.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos or files.
  • No chat or messaging between players.

6. Security

The game makes no network connections of its own. Everything that leaves the device goes through Google's software over encrypted (TLS) connections, and unencrypted connections are disabled in the app.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; all advertising is child-directed and non-personalized as described in section 2. A parent or guardian who has a question about a child's data can contact the developer (section 9).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
